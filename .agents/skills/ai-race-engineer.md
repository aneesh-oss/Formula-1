# AI Race Engineer Skill: Autonomous Telemetry Intelligence

This skill governs the integration of Large Language Models (LLMs) with the PitWall OS analytical engine to generate real-time race debriefs and answer natural language strategy questions.

---

## 1. AI Race Engineer Topology
The AI Race Engineer acts as an analytical agent bridging natural language and SQL queries:

```text
[User Prompt: "Why was Norris faster than Verstappen through Parabolica?"]
                           │
                           ▼
             [AI Race Engineer Agent]
                           │
           (Tool Calling: query_corner_telemetry)
                           │
                           ▼
               [In-Process DuckDB Client]
                           │
       (Scans: data/lakehouse/2024/monza/R/*.parquet)
                           │
                           ▼
               [Synthesizes Telemetry Delta]
  - Norris braked 12m later (Distance 5120m vs 5108m)
  - Norris apex speed: 214 km/h vs Verstappen 208 km/h (+6 km/h)
  - Time delta gained through turn: -0.142s
                           │
                           ▼
          [Natural Language Audio/Text Debrief]
```

---

## 2. Agent Tool Definitions
The AI Race Engineer exposes structured functions to LLMs:
1. `get_driver_pace_summary(year, circuit, session, driver)`: Returns median lap time, stint length, and tire wear status.
2. `compare_corner_telemetry(year, circuit, session, driver_a, driver_b, corner_number)`: Extracts distance-aligned delta through specific turns.
3. `simulate_pit_strategy(current_lap, gap_to_leader, compound, current_tire_life)`: Runs pit window optimization.

---

## 3. Grounding & Anti-Hallucination Invariant
- Every natural language statement generated by the AI Race Engineer **MUST be grounded in raw values** returned by DuckDB SQL queries. Never speculate on lap times, braking points, or tire pressures without citing query data.
